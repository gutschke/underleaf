# The landing — how "nobody did this" is made to work

*DM-only. Written 2026-08-30. **Read this whenever you schedule anything in Acts
II–III.** The ending of this campaign is a reveal of an ABSENCE, and absences do
not land the way reveals of presence do.*

---

## The problem, stated precisely

**Every unresolved thread at the moment of the reveal reads as a clue still owed.
Clues imply a clue-giver.**

So what matters at Ep 24 is not how many mysteries were opened across the
campaign — it is **how many are still live in the players' hands on the night.**

> **An absence lands when the field is emptying. It fails when the field is still
> filling** — because *"nobody did this"* becomes indistinguishable from *"the DM
> is withholding,"* **and a teenage table will read it as the second one, every
> time.**

**Retired threads are fuel for the landing. Live threads are debt against it.**

**The multi-cause discipline is excellent for texture and actively hostile to the
landing.** Both are true. Manage them as separate concerns: hold causes open for
richness *during* play, and make sure the visible thread count is **going down**
across Acts II–III.

---

## What overloaded looks like at this table

**Not confusion. Simplification.**

They will not say *this is too complicated.* They will say nothing, and quietly
collapse the entire field onto one person. **They have already done it three
times — {{pc:4}}, then Bea, then Farallon Mutual Casualty.**

Right now that is the healthy version. **At session 24 it means picking whoever
they suspected last, deciding it was them, and being flatly unsatisfied when told
no.**

**The tells, in order of appearance:**

1. They stop theorising and start asking the DM to summarise.
2. **The loudest player's theory becomes the party's theory with no dissent.**
   *(Watch this one. {{pc:4}}'s player is currently the table's theory engine, and
   nobody contradicted his insurance-company read.)*
3. They mine scenes for the object rather than playing the scene.
4. Disengagement surfaces as jokes and phone-checking — **never as a complaint.**

**By the time a teenager tells you a campaign is too complicated, they have been
checked out for four sessions.**

## The structure: demonstrate → state → re-demonstrate → grieve

**Demonstrations make absence land. Statements do not.**

### 1. Demonstrate first, and let a PLAYER name it

**Ep 6's archive failure is a demonstration** — the finding aid points at a
*person* in informal custody, not a shelf. **Get a player to say out loud what
that means before Ep 8.**

> **Currently the ordering is slightly wrong.** Nia states the thesis at Ep 8
> before the party has run enough failed searches with their own hands to feel it.
> Stated too early it is heard as **one NPC's opinion**, and gets overruled by the
> next clue. **Make Nia confirm an experience rather than introduce a claim.**

### 2. State it once, at the hinge

Ep 8, Nia. **It lands once and is not repeated.**

### 3. Re-demonstrate, and make the thread count visibly fall

**Ep 12 retires B1 on the evidence floor** — Rosa's ledger names no other keeper,
ever, across nearly fifty years — **whether or not any player says the recognition line.**
That is the model: the artifact does the work.

**Two demonstrations across twenty-five sessions is thin. Add a third, early:**
the **S1 flattening drought in Eps 9–14** is exactly where it belongs, and fixing
that drought is the single highest-leverage change available. **S1 is the only
mechanism teaching *nobody in the chain intends this* before Act III.**

**Then retire threads ON SCREEN across Eps 15–18.** Pick three, plus B1 at Ep 12:
T7 at Ep 6 · B23/B18 at Ep 9 · B22 at ~Ep 17.

**Ep 24 must not be the first time the party hears "nobody did this" applied to
their own case. It should be the third or fourth.**

### 4. Grieve — and this is the part that is currently missing

**Cognition alone will not carry this for thirteen-year-olds.**

Someone has to say plainly that **they wanted there to be someone too.**

> **Rosa is the right mouth for it.** She is the only person the party will meet
> who had this and *stopped* — and a woman who spent nearly fifty years believing she was
> the only one is the one person whose disappointment is earned rather than
> supplied.

**Without the grief beat the reveal is a correction. With it, it is an
experience.**

---

## Protect Farallon Mutual Casualty

**It is the best asset the campaign has for this landing, and the party built it
themselves.**

They invented an institution to explain a pattern that has no institution behind
it — unprompted, in Ep 5, and nobody contradicted it.

**The moment at Ep 24 when they realise they made the company up is a better
payoff than any line an NPC could deliver.**

**So: name it, make it real and boring and dead, never decide whether it means
anything, and never give it a second scene.** Rosa has never worked in insurance
and will say so plainly if asked — a genuine disconfirming data point on one limb,
which makes the theory *falsifiable in principle without being falsified.* That is
better for the party than a wall.

## The register problem, which may be more dangerous than the complexity

**Eps 20, 22, 23 and 24 are four consecutive HEAVY sessions whose only verb is
"talk to someone." Ten of the last eleven sessions are go-meet-an-interesting-
person.**

**For this age bracket that is the more dangerous of the two failure modes.**
Ep 23's three-question dream-channel puzzle is the only structural variation in
eleven sessions.

**Break them up.** The same discipline that produced Ep 6's shape — change the
verb, not the room — applies to the whole back half.

---

## And the calendar, which is the real constraint

**The mystery load is fine. The session load is over.**

- **23–27 sessions against a 25-session plan.** Nine of seventeen authored
  sessions are HEAVY. Real absorbable slack among the ★ sessions is **zero**.
- **Recap is unbooked. 10–20 minutes × 20 sessions is two entire missing
  sessions.** Book it or cut for it.
- Four buffers are consumed arithmetically before anything slips.

**Every held thread eventually asks for thirty seconds of screen time to stay
warm.** That is how mystery load becomes calendar load, and the calendar is what
will actually bite.

**Recommendation: convert one more HEAVY into a real deletion before Act II
opens.** Eps 10 and 12 are the peak-juggling sessions — four NPCs and seven or
eight threads each — and **Ep 10 is the more compressible.**
