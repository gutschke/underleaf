# Underleaf — episode outline (Eps 5-25)

> [!CAUTION]
> **DM-ONLY — FULL CAMPAIGN SPOILERS**
> Session-by-session horizon through the projected end of the campaign. If you are or might become a player, close this file now.

> [!WARNING]
> **This is a LIVING DOCUMENT. Edits are expected.**
>
> The outline exists to keep the DM honest when planning individual episodes — not to constrain creative storytelling by players. **Perfect is the enemy of good.** An outline set in stone would stifle emergent play; an outline that is never revisited would drift into irrelevance. The right level of commitment is:
>
> - **The overall arc landing is load-bearing** — cosmology, invariants, closing image, mirror-substrate revelation, three-act shape. These do not change (see [`big-arc.md`](big-arc.md) locked invariants).
> - **The specific session-by-session shape is a working guess** — drafted 2026-08-17 from a three-expert audit against played Eps 1-3 + drafted Ep 4. Every future episode's played result will inform revisions here. Some sessions will land as drafted; some will compress or extend; some will get replaced entirely by what the players make possible.
> - **The DM's discipline:** re-read this file when planning each new episode. Ask: which arc-nodes are due? Which have slipped? Which have been overtaken by what happened at the table? Adjust the outline. Do not obey it.
>
> Play IS canon. This outline yields to play.

---

## Purpose

This file exists because Underleaf has an ambitious ~25-session arc that must land specific structural beats (per-PC Phase-2 realizations, Act I→II network hinge, institutional trinity, Act III conversation, closing image). Without a horizon, individual-episode planning drifts and the DM discovers by Ep 12 that they have only covered Ep-3-4-worth of arc.

**The outline's job is to prevent that drift.** It is not a script.

## Legend

- **LOAD**: LIGHT = 0-1 arc-node + texture (recovery/rest); MEDIUM = 2 arc-nodes; HEAVY = 3 arc-nodes (compress if the session slips)
- **★** = deliberate slack session (no Phase change or Act hinge)
- **Arc-node**: what STRUCTURALLY has to happen — one-liner in structural terms, not plot terms
- Hook IDs reference [`hook-ledger.md`](hook-ledger.md) (U/A/B/C/T/S categories)

## Sub-arc discipline reminder

Per 2026-08-17 Expert 3 audit, three DISTRACTOR-RISK sub-arcs to watch:

1. **Mira-Kai brother arc** — highest alt-arc risk. Fed by holding the DEFAULT-LOST answer in `facts-ledger.md` §31 (Kai is truly lost; no records anywhere; Mira and her note are the only carriers). The tragic-but-bearable read is the campaign's ending for Mira's sub-arc; occasional absence-outline breadcrumbs (~1-2 per act) are texture, not investigation. Do NOT improv a shred, a database entry, or a reveal at the table — the discipline is *when Mira asks, the answer is nothing*. The "reserved card" (revisit if a perfect situation earns it) is a design-cycle decision, not a table decision.
2. **Dakota-as-plot-driver** — fixable per-session via S2 discipline (hook-ledger). Requires active DM intervention through Ep 8.
3. **Ocean Beach teens** — keep as canary-group only. No in-person scenes past Ep 9. If Ren gets promoted to a caster, it's Act III closing-image texture, not a plotline.

Per user directive: "*sub arcs are great and should be encouraged. but they need to fit into the bigger theme and cosmology, and we can dwell indefinitely on a subarc that doesn't achieve anything.*" Every FEEDER sub-arc's payoff advances a main-arc pillar (cosmology recognition, PC magic realization, institutional/Companion emergence, or Act III conversation). If a sub-arc runs 2+ sessions without visibly feeding a pillar, it's likely becoming an alt-arc — retire or converge.
