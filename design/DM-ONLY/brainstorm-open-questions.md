## Non-human-intelligence intermission episode

**Status:** active-brainstorm (2026-08-17)
**Might implement:** somewhere Act II mid-late, possibly as texture between heavy institutional-arc sessions, or as a dedicated interlude outside the 25-session count
**Might discard:** if the world-building overhead exceeds what a one-off can carry, or if it reads as gimmick/derailment

### The seed

One episode where the DM hands the party character sheets NOT for their usual PCs but for **non-human intelligent structures** — quarks, galaxies, sequoias, a crow murder. Connects thematically to the campaign's misdirection seed bank (galaxies-sentient, animals-sense-magic per `big-arc.md` §Misdirection seed bank) — the payoff being that we CANNOT meaningfully communicate across these scales.

*"How would you even talk to a quark about its social life? How would you ask a galaxy to use dark matter or black holes to physically grab something — or even to huddle together with other galaxies — if intentional physical movement is a foreign concept?"* (— user framing, 2026-08-17)

### The design challenge — the trap to avoid

**"Quarks are just humans with different skins" is the failure mode.** An intermission that lands has to feel:

- **Surreal.** Not "aliens with different biology"; genuinely non-human cognition.
- **Non-interacting with the world as we know it.** Humans invisible or barely register. Human artifacts (buildings, technology, language) either don't exist in the play frame or exist as environmental facts the players can't relate to as intentional.
- **Self-consistent internally.** Social interactions foreign to human minds but coherent to whatever the players are. Rules that make sense in-fiction even if unrecognizable to outside observers.
- **World-building-heavy.** A lot of prep for one session.

### Underleaf-canon connection

Vindicates the "loneliness at every emergent scale" texture (`big-arc.md` §Loose threads that survive the campaign — the misdirection map "adds together to tell a quiet story about loneliness at every emergent scale"). Reinforces the cosmological stance that intelligence at different scales *cannot easily talk*. The intermission would be the party *briefly living* what Act III's recognition of Companion articulates — encountering intelligent shape you cannot fully understand.

### Risks

- **Falls flat.** Players stare at each other trying to figure out what a quark even does.
- **Feels gimmicky.** Reads as derailment rather than a meaningful side-trip.
- **Insufficient world-building.** The prep required is real and easy to under-invest in.
- **Session-budget cost.** The 25-session arc doesn't have slack for a full-session interlude unless it also carries main-arc weight.

### Open questions to refine over time

1. **Which non-human?** Quarks / galaxies / a sequoia / a crow murder — each has radically different play-shape and world-building needs. Pick one or offer variants? Player choice?
2. **When?** Act II slack session? Act III recognition-interval? A dedicated interlude session *outside* the main count (so it doesn't cost against the ~25 budget)?
3. **How much world-building can be delegated?** Could the players themselves collaboratively build the non-human world during the session (share the cognitive lift; makes the surreal feel collectively-authored)?
4. **Memory persistence.** Do the PCs keep any memory of the intermission when they return? What does *"you were briefly a galaxy"* mean afterward? A dream? A texture? A note in a facts-ledger they can't quite parse?
5. **Thematic-adjacent connection.** Which main-arc beat does the intermission sit next to? Before the flattening reveal (foreshadowing loneliness-at-every-scale)? After the AI acknowledges The Quiet (mirror of Companion's own encounter with something-it-cannot-name)? During grief for Vivian (grief-at-scales)?
6. **Rules frame.** Adapt quire-v0.1 stats/mechanics (map STR/DEX to something surreal) OR invent a one-off mechanic for the intermission? The invention has more surreal potential but more prep cost.
7. **Payoff for the framing.** If the intermission lands, what does the DM want the players to *carry back* into their PC play? A humility? A specific new capacity? A memory-shape? Or is the intermission its own reward without carry-back?

## Morgan-father-timeline seam (9y-SSN-hit vs 2y-disappearance)

**Status:** active-brainstorm (2026-08-22 — flagged during Ep 4 played)
**Might implement:** commit a working answer by Ep 6-7 planning, before the party's Sacramento or Berkeley threads force an improv
**Might discard:** N/A — this is a canon reconciliation, not an add-on; the answer must be picked, not dropped

### The seed

Atticus (Morgan's player) noticed at the Ep 4 table that Detective Ortiz's voicemail (Ep 3 close, replayed Ep 4 Scene 1 Beat 2) cites a paperwork echo from **nine years ago** — but per Morgan's PC-sheet backstory, the father *"missed a birthday, then a phone call, then all of them"* only **two years ago**. That's a ~7-year gap the DM has not yet reconciled in canon. The player noticed — favorably. He read it not as a bug but as "cool, this is a campaign where time and cause/effect can be surprising."

### Why this matters

- **Not a bug — arc-load-bearing raw material.** Underleaf cosmology already permits things being set in motion years before their visible effects (retro-causal edits at cost per world-truths; anchor-site permeability per anchors-cards-ledgers). A 7-year gap between the SSN paperwork and the disappearance is *exactly* the kind of temporal-shape the campaign already knows how to hold.
- **The player is primed.** Atticus flagged it in-fiction/at-table without complaint. If the DM lands a good answer in the next 2-3 sessions, it converts from "seam" to "aha — the DM was tracking this the whole time" — a trust win.
- **If ignored:** the seam calcifies into an unresolved DM-continuity issue that erodes trust in the DM's coherence. By Ep 12-14 (mid-Act-II) the whole party will have Morgan-father Sacramento material to whiteboard against; the discrepancy must have a held answer by then.

### The four candidate reads (mirrored from facts-ledger §6 TODO)

- **(a) Father-was-already-in-motion.** The 2017 hit = the moment father's colleagues privately flagged his security-adjacent past to him. He kept working for 7 years but was making arrangements the whole time. 2024 disappearance = *completion*, not *beginning*. Cleanest mundane read; no cosmology needed.
- **(b) Substrate-anchored 7-year lag.** The substrate's cheapest self-consistent path for the father's disappearance required a 2017 paperwork anchor that the 2024 vanishing settles onto. Anchor-site permeability, but time-shaped rather than place-shaped. Feeds Phase-2 pattern-recognition beautifully — Priya/Mira/Morgan can *read* the temporal shape once they see it.
- **(c) Two separate events.** The 2017 hit isn't him at all — it's a name-and-partial-SSN collision that the substrate later exploits as *cover* for his 2024 vanishing. Ortiz's literal *"I'm not saying it was him"* becomes true. Lowest cosmological cost; loses the "fate-set-in-motion-earlier" pull.
- **(d) Retrocausal-self shape.** The 2017 echo *becomes* the father's after-the-fact when he disappears in 2024 — the substrate's cheapest past for his vanishing routes through an already-attested 2017 SSN anomaly. Fits big-arc's *"future actions can retrocausally condition present ones"* frame. Ties to the same reading-list as §1 (1878 ledger) and §31 (Kai's erasure).

### Design considerations

1. **Which read serves the campaign best?** (b) and (d) most reinforce cosmology; (a) is safest; (c) is escape-hatch.
2. **Timing of commit.** Cross-refs the S3 institutional-frame redirect discipline — an Ortiz session that fires this reconciliation would also fire S3. Consider bundling.
3. **Player-facing framing.** Ortiz should acknowledge the discrepancy quietly (*"Yeah — that caught my eye too. I don't know what to make of it either"*) without explaining. The 7-year gap stays open until the DM commits to a reading.
4. **How does the reveal cash out?** Options: Morgan finds his father alive (per §6 canon) in a state consistent with the read (e.g., under (a) father says *"they warned me back in 2017 and I've been planning ever since"*; under (b) his disappearance-shape *fits* the substrate pattern the party is learning to read; under (d) his father genuinely does not know why he vanished when he did — *"it just felt like time"*).
5. **Cross-linked to S3.** The Argonaut Records Holdings LLC (§27) dissolved 1998, was the trust vehicle for Dakota's Sacramento locker. If the 2017 hit is a records-cross-match with the LLC (as one of the Ep 5 alternates proposes), the father's timeline seam becomes the *bridge* between the personal (Morgan's arc) and the institutional (S3 frame).
