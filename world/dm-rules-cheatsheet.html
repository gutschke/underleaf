Underleaf DM Rules Cheatsheet

Quire v0.1 rules reference · two-page double-sided

Core Resolution — 2d6 + stat + skill

Roll (+mods)Result
≤ 6Miss — fail, or succeed at cost
7-9Partial — yes-but, yes-and-cost
10+Hit — clean success
12+Exceptional — success with bonus

Wild outcomes — check the dice AS DICE, before modifiers:

  • Double 6s → exceptional regardless of math; player narrates an unexpected positive detail
  • Double 1s → mechanical result resolves normally with mods; DM introduces an unexpected complication or twist

DMs never roll. Players roll for uncertain outcomes; you narrate.

Cap: +2 from skills (one category + one tag, or two stacking tags), plus stat.

Harm (4-box track)

#StateEffect
1BruisedNo penalty
2Wounded−1 to physical rolls
3Serious−1 to ALL rolls; needs care soon
4CriticalOut. Dying or unconscious.

Healing is mostly fictional. Box 1 rest; Box 2 days; Box 3 medical; Box 4 significant.

Stress (4-box track)

#StateEffect
1TenseNo penalty
2Strained−1 to WIS rolls
3Frayed−2 WIS; thread debt accumulates faster
4BrokenNo casting; −1 to ALL rolls

Recovery: 1 box / night's sleep; 1 box / meaningful conversation-art-connection; never in-scene.

Combat (theater of the mind; DM turn-orders naturally)

Moves (anyone):

  • Attack — Action vs target. Hit deals harm; Partial deals harm + you take some too.
  • Defend — DEX/Action against incoming. Hit avoids; Partial reduces.
  • Maneuver — change position / create advantage. Hit gives ally +1 to their next roll.
  • Aid — help another's action directly. Hit gives +1.
  • Press — force someone to reveal/give up. Influence-based.
  • Withdraw — disengage. Hit clean; Partial at cost.

If tempo spikes: DM may call Insight; high rolls act first. Combat is rare and lethal; PCs are mostly civilians.

Magic — plausibility tiers (DM adjudicates)

TierRoll?Outcome
FreeNoSucceeds; no debt
CheapNo (WIS check for show)Succeeds; minimal debt
Costly2d6+WIS10+ clean; 7-9 partial (higher cost than expected); ≤6 fails + debt anyway. +1 stress.
Hard2d6+WIS with −1/−2 penalty10+ partial only; 7-9 fail with major debt; ≤6 backlash. +2 stress.
Prohibited—Not allowed

Foci shift tier down by ONE within domain (Costly→Cheap, Hard→Costly; Prohibited stays). Foci can break/fade/transform on Hard casts.

Free/Cheap spam triggers a stress check after 3rd-4th in a scene. Roll 2d6+WIS; miss marks stress.

Thread debt (narrative meter, NOT a number)

StateThe world is doing
QuietNothing notable
NoticedSmall "lucky timing" comments
WatchedCoincidences accumulate around caster; banks flag, friends ask
Pushing BackBad luck for the caster specifically; harder casts
HuntedWorld actively obstructs; severe. Can't easily cast.

Narrate the state in fiction ("things have eased; the world's eye has drifted"), never by number. Eases with restraint.

Advancement (session end)

Each player may mark up to ONE:

  • ☐ Resolved a hard moment in line with alignment
  • ☐ Learned something true about The Quiet, world, or another character
  • ☐ Took a meaningful risk for someone else
  • ☐ Acted against own short-term interest because story demanded it
  • ☐ Took a complication/scar that will matter later

Every 5 marks → 1 advancement (stat +1 cap +3; category mastery +1→+2; new tag; new focus; NPC relationship; resource). Cap: 8 advancements per character.

Alignment

Standard 9-grid. Chosen at creation, binding — PCs act in accord even when short-term costly.

Drift tracker: DM privately notes actions that pulled toward a different quadrant. ~5 drift marks toward one destination → realignment conversation. Old vows feel wrong; old allies notice.

Silent-player firewall — NEVER SAY at the table

magic · The Quiet · casting · cast · spell · premonition · psychic · chosen · destiny · awakening · the elect · sixth sense · thaumaturgy · sorcery

Instead say: coincidence · luck · timing · weird · uncanny.

Three lines memorized:

  • "is this magic?" → "Could be. Could not be. What does your character do?"
  • "what does it MEAN?" → "What do you think it means?" (then listen)
  • "AI? Conspiracy?" → same as #1. Do NOT affirm; the theory must come from players.

DM-only: magic-discovery arc (Underleaf-specific)

Phase 1 — Accidental (sessions 1-3): players have no cast on their sheets; DM silently grants Free/Cheap effects to strong intent, attributed to luck. Log in accidental-cast log.
Phase 2 — Realization (scripted, ~session 3-5): DM reveals the pattern. Player gains cast mechanics on their sheet.
Phase 3 — Trying-too-hard tax (~2-3 sessions after): intentional casts take −2 until PC has "one quiet moment of release" — an unforced spontaneous cast they didn't aim for. Drops tax to 0.
Each PC realizes on their own schedule. Release is the cue for the act break in Underleaf's three-act structure.

Foci — quick DM reminder

Cognitive anchor, not a magic battery. Personal, idiosyncratic — a phrase, tool, breath count, song lyric, object.

Domain: emotion, systems/information, identity, motion, knowledge, body/care, memory/identity, speech/presence, posture/presence, etc.

Effect: shifts in-domain casts down one tier. Out-of-domain: no help.

Can break, fade, corrupt, or transform when spent on Hard casts.
