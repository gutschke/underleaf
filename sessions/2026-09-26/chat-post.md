**Session 7 — The Fourth Birthday**

Mei turned four at Coyote Point, and you brought presents. {{pc:4}} brought a
*toddler's first camping set* — pop-up tent, flickering fake campfire, the works —
and a big bouquet for Yui, because he feels terrible about a bag he still can't
explain. {{pc:3}} crocheted a birthday cake with a large **4** on it and brought a
real spoon to go with it. {{pc:2}} brought four one-dollar bills, and was told by
everybody that four is the unlucky number, and did not care.

The parking lot then charged exactly **$4**. {{pc:2}} refused.
{{pc:3}} paid.

**On the drive down, {{pc:4}} called Detective Ortiz** — who said she'd been just
about to call him, and couldn't say why. *In California you can ask for your
personnel file in writing, and they have thirty days.* **That's exactly what a
relationship advancement is for.** {{pc:2}} has Alaia and {{pc:1}} has Trina, and
they work the same way: say "I call…" and they pick up.

---

**{{pc:5}} remembered, at the edge of a playground, that he was carrying a gun.**
He considered the museum bathroom and a bush three feet from the slides before walking back to the car, and {{pc:4}} came along for company. **You then head-butted
each other walking side by side**, a few steps from the car, in front of the toddlers at the fence. *"You walk like my sister. She's one."*

{{pc:5}} went into the playground to have it out with them and lost. He told one
that Santa isn't real. *"That's what my ten-year-old brother said. He didn't get
any presents that year. He never said it again."* So {{pc:5}} shouted, for the
whole park: ***"Kids, if you ever need gifts, look under your parents' bed on
Valentine's Day."***

**Several parents are now in possession of video.**

Yui arrived at exactly that moment with a tray of lumpia, decided he must be one
of Reggie's friends, and asked him quietly not to look under her bed. She laughed
all the way to the picnic tables.

---

Reggie, at the grill, says life got a lot better not long after the In-N-Out. He
still gets the 5×5, but a new roommate from culinary school has them all eating
salads, and he's thinking about a hospitality degree. {{pc:2}} told him the airline
might pay for it. He's thinking about that too.

**Somebody yelled "SNAKE!"** {{pc:2}} arrived first, holding a large rock. It was
CuriOdyssey's rubber boa, and the boy had been allowed to hold it.

**{{pc:4}} ran Simon Says.** He opened by telling the mother who'd drafted him that
his name happens to be Simon, and asking hers; she informed him she was married
and left. The toddlers didn't understand the rules, one had a diaper situation, and
the mother gave him a big thumbs-up from across the lawn at his lowest moment.
**Then he worked out that they'll do anything after "Simon says"**, and used it to
have the toddlers take {{pc:5}} down in a dog pile. It was a great success. He switched to
silly instructions after that.

**{{pc:5}} is now "Mezzanine."** A kid ran off with his keychain; he yelled for his
magazine; they misheard. He got it back with his shoulder, off his heel, and **into
his teeth**, to the whole lawn chanting his new name. Then someone handed him a
piñata rope, and he stood holding it for quite a while before he worked out what
it was — and the littlest ones kept wandering over to stand next to him instead of
hitting the thing.

**The cake said HAPPY 3rd.** Every adult agreed it was fine. Mei did not. {{pc:1}}
noticed, and fixed it with a food-safe marker, and got **a high four.** A parent
told him he was good with kids and asked if he had family nearby; he mentioned his
dad was missing, and the parent's joke died halfway.

{{pc:3}} looked at the photo board. One picture had somebody cut out of it. Yui
did that, with scissors. {{pc:3}} left it there.

**After cake, Mei choked on a grape.** It makes no sound. **{{pc:2}} had it out
before anyone else had noticed**, and then sat with Yui on the blanket, and couldn't eat a thing. Later she took a toothpick-sized splinter out of
a small boy with the kit she carries everywhere, and he stayed curled up next to
her for a while.

---

In the parking lot {{pc:4}} finally told Yui about her file, and apologised, and
kept saying *"Don't let them give you an excerpt."* Inez took his notes off him to
make sure Yui remembers. Mei-Mei was asleep.

**And then {{pc:3}}'s phone buzzed:**

> *"hey!! sorry sorry sorry. back in town. saturday the 27th, lunch, noon-ish? I'll
> send you where that morning. bring whoever"*

{{pc:4}} has since updated his campaign website. Wealth distribution is today's
problem. Donations welcome. Still no word on which city.

**Next: Saturday 27 March, lunch, around noon.** Nia will say where that morning.
