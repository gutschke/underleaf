## What landed, in one breath

The recap page was read in full and **much appreciated**. The midweek messages
went out a few days early: **no replies, but every player read them.** All five
arc-nodes the episode carried fired, most of them without a prompt, and the
comedy came from the players, not the guide.

## Before the party

**Gifts, all player-invented:**
- **{{pc:4}}**: a *toddler's first camping set* — a pop-up tent for a bedroom, a
  flickering fake campfire, kid-sized accessories — **and a big bouquet for Yui.**
  He doesn't understand how his bag got her into trouble, since he doesn't know
  what happened at the gate either, **but he feels horrible about it.**
- **{{pc:3}}**: a crocheted, toddler-sized birthday-cake stuffy with a prominent
  **4**, and a real toddler spoon to go with it.
- **{{pc:2}}**: had not planned a gift and pulled out **four $1 bills**. Everyone
  pointed out that four is unlucky in Chinese culture; she was undeterred.

**The Ortiz call — after ONE light prompt.** The players had just remarked,
unprompted, that they knew about their allies; **{{pc:4}} still didn't connect
that this was a situation one could help with** until the first-rung prompt. Ortiz
was very friendly, **said she had just been about to call him, and didn't know
why**, was delighted to hear from him, gave him the Labor Code, and had little
else. **{{pc:1}}'s father never came up.**

> **HELD OPEN — "I was just about to call you."** A cheap coincidence, or
> nothing. Never explained; never repeated as a pattern.

**The parking lot wanted exactly $4.** {{pc:2}} refused to pay it. An exasperated
{{pc:3}} paid cash instead. 

## The gun, the headbutt, and the karens

Reggie waved at them, showing a range of emotions as he recognised the party. **{{pc:5}}, on the
DM's prompt, realised he was about to carry a pistol into a children's party.** He
did not want to walk the forty-five seconds back; considered the CuriOdyssey
bathroom (further than the car) and the bushes (three feet from the adventure
playground, in plain sight). **{{pc:4}} offered to walk back with him.**

**Both rolled very badly on DEX and head-butted each other walking side by side**,
steps from the car. Four children at the playground fence laughed: *"You can't
even walk."* *"You walk like my sister, and she's one."* {{pc:5}} locked the gun in the car and went into the playground after them; the mockery came from
everywhere and he could not place it. He told the one he thought he had spotted
that neither the Easter Bunny nor Santa is real. The child, deadpan: *"That's what
my ten-year-old brother said. He didn't get any presents for his birthday or
Christmas that year. He never said it again."*

{{pc:5}} shouted: ***"Kids, if you ever need gifts, look under your
parents' bed on Valentine's Day."*** **Several parents turned round, outraged, and
filmed him on their phones.**

**Yui arrived at exactly that moment**, handed {{pc:5}} a big tray of lumpia,
said he must be one of Reggie's friends, and told him quietly that **he shouldn't
look under her and Inez's bed.** She could not stop chuckling as she walked
{{pc:5}} and {{pc:4}} over to the picnic area.

> **The phone videos exist.** A clip of {{pc:5}} shouting about Valentine's Day is
> on several strangers' phones. **Not a plan, not a threat** — see hook-ledger T29.

## The afternoon

**Yui**, across several stretches of small talk: explained Mei's name, told her side of the bag story, and was delighted
with the flowers.

**Reggie**, making hot dogs for the kids: **life has got much better "since
shortly after" the In-N-Out.** He still gets the 5×5 from his roommate the
manager, but no longer lives on it five days a week, struggling on the days the manager is off. **A new, additional roommate, a culinary student**, has the flat eating better; his new favourite is **an avocado salad
with puffed buckwheat**. He has been saving hard for years because he wants to *do
something with his life*; United was his first job out of high school, and he is
thinking about **a hospitality degree.** {{pc:2}} pointed out that United might
pay for it; he liked that and worried about being tied to them afterwards.

> **HELD OPEN — why his life improved "shortly after" meeting them.** Ordinary
> luck, the new roommate, or something else. **Never explained.** Reggie had lines
> tonight, which the guide did not plan; that is fine and it stays texture.

**The snake scare landed.** {{pc:2}} ran to where the boy pointed, **picked up a
big rock**, and was ready to deal with whatever was in the bushes by CuriOdyssey
— until the boy's father stepped out, asked if she'd seen his son, realised the
confusion, and explained about the rubber boa.

**Simon Says — {{pc:4}}.** A tired mother drafted him. **He said it was great,
because his name happens to be Simon, and asked hers** — she stared, told him she
was obviously married, and walked off. *(He had not meant to flirt; it came out
wrong.)* The toddlers didn't understand the rules; one left after a diaper
incident; the mother gave him a cheerful thumbs-up from across the lawn **at the
exact moment he was most frustrated.** Then he noticed the toddlers do whatever
follows the key phrase — **and used his new power to have them trip {{pc:5}}**,
*because Simon said so.* They dog-piled him and had a blast. {{pc:4}} realised his
mistake and went to toddler rules. *(Along the way he had them "follow that individual"; {{pc:3}} explained that "individual" just means "person", and one child cheerfully spent the rest of the afternoon telling everyone that **"immolate" means person.** {{pc:3}} tried everything and gave up; the mispronunciation stuck.)* The rules: every instruction starts "Simon says", every
instruction is silly. They loved it, until they got bored and ran off.

**{{pc:5}} and "Mezzanine".** Showing off to some four- to six-year-olds, he took
out his backup focus, the magazine-shaped keychain. A child grabbed it and ran.
*"Give me back my magazine!"* — **and the kids had a name for him: "Mezzanine."**
They played hacky sack with it until he waded in; **on a good roll he intercepted
it with his shoulder, bounced it off the back of his heel, and caught it in his
teeth.** Everybody cheered ***"MEZZANINE!"*** at the top of their lungs.

**Then the piñata.** Somebody handed him a string. He stood for minutes, trying to
look relaxed, unable to move, sit or lean — **until he realised it was the piñata
rope.** He pulled it up; the kids played, **but mostly they wanted to mill around
him instead of hitting it.** He didn't understand it, and enjoyed it. **He got the
slip.**

**The Mandarin line.** **{{pc:1}}'s player delivered the call to Mei-Mei in
Mandarin** at the cake.

**The cake — {{pc:1}}.** He noticed **Mei's distress** at the wrong number,
realised she had been perfectly clear on three versus four while the adults said
otherwise, **and fixed it with a food-safe Sharpie.** Mei, relieved, gave him **a
high four.** A parent praised his sensitivity — *"some adults can be so dismissive
of little kids; you're good with kids. Do you have family in the area?"* — and he
**volunteered that his father had gone missing**, no more. He smiled wryly when the
parent began *"so he just went out to get some m— oh, I'm so sorry, I can't
believe I made that joke."* **The father thread breathed. No information, no
lead.**

**The photo board — {{pc:3}}.** She tried to read who had been cut out and got
only a generic woman — **much what Yui had already volunteered.** In the DM's first account *"her body didn't react much"*; clarified afterwards: **a mild headache.** **The scene with
Yui happened; she got a mild headache; she did not push again after a "useless"
reading, and moved on.**

> **The DM's read, and it matters for the finale:** she probably connected the
> headache to trying, without saying so (**the players have been speculating out of
> game**). **More broadly: the players seem to have concluded that if they push,
> the world won't do it — it happens when it happens, or it doesn't.** They don't
> see the whole pattern, and may not be able to. **Next session's handout states
> the aiming rule outright; it will be confirming something they already feel.**

**The grape — {{pc:2}}.** Mei sat on the blanket beside her and choked. **She
moved at once — no prompt, no waiting for anyone.** The grape was out almost as
soon as her arms were round Mei. Yui ran over and reacted as a stressed parent
does, fixing on the wrong thing while she caught up; then sat down next to
{{pc:2}}, **and the small talk helped them both.** {{pc:2}} **lost her appetite
completely** — out of character for her, and she noticed — and **expects to sleep
badly, again.** Yui sympathised and was endlessly grateful.

**The splinter.** A sobbing boy — dropped off by a parent who asked *"are you the nurse?"* and then left — with **a splinter the size of a toothpick** in his hand. {{pc:2}} took it out easily, disinfected it, and did all the things her
training says, **from the supplies in her satchel — her focus, the bag she carries everywhere.** He
cuddled up to her, still wincing now and then. She would have offered pain relief,
but the parent had gone.

> **She never explained any of it. Like the snake, it was almost on auto-pilot: it
> had to happen, and she did it.** **Her moment of release was the small talk with
> Yui afterwards** (DM's ruling). The slips' content reached her, some by slip and
> some told.

**The Rosa question never came up.**

## The parking lot

**{{pc:4}} finally got Yui's attention** and told her about the Labor Code, apologising
all the while for getting her into trouble. **She was grateful and thoroughly
confused**; it was all news, and nothing like what her union rep had said. He kept
insisting on the important bit: *"Don't let them give you just an excerpt — you are
entitled to the full file."* **Inez**, carrying a sleeping Mei, offered to take his
scribbled notes: *"Give me those. I know my girl; she'll never remember all this.
I'll make sure she knows what to do, and I'll remind her tomorrow."*

Everyone waved; the cars left. **Nia's message arrived on {{pc:3}}'s phone**, which
surprised the table for a moment and was quickly explained away.

**{{pc:4}} closed the session by updating his campaign website** — *wealth
distribution is today's problem, donate to my campaign*, and more in that vein —
**still without naming the city he wants to be mayor of.** He plans **QR codes
for the site all over the place**, and briefly tried to get the children on the
adventure playground to contribute, until Yui led them away.
