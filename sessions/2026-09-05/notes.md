# Ep 6 — Eight Twenty Sharp · played record

*Written up 2026-09-05, from the session. **Gaps are marked TO CONFIRM** — the DM

## What the design got right, and it was not the mechanic

**The table reenacted the dance.** Standing up, turning each other, doing the
figures. **Nothing in the design asked for that and it was the best thing in the
session.** Whatever the next version of the minigame is, it has to leave room for
people to get out of their chairs.

**The hall under-delivered and that turned out to be correct.** Questions got
shouted, answers arrived out of order and incomplete, and the players
reconstructed a coherent picture afterwards from their own notes. **The
reconstruction was the fun, not the delivery.** The long scene at Rosa's table
then closed whatever was still open, properly.

## What the mechanic got wrong

**See `episodes/006-eight-twenty-sharp/props/PLAYTEST-EP6.md`** for the full
verdict. In short: **the per-PC progressive arcs did not survive contact.** DO
THIS cards fired out of position; several played an encounter out completely and
then later cards in the same stack contradicted it. **The ADOPTED deck never came
up at all — its second failure in two runs.** The run-guide did not carry the DM
through the mechanic either. **Both need another editing pass and simulated runs
before this is reused.**

**The Alaia trade never fired, and that was our fault.** The Ep 5 recap ended with
*"do not mention Alaia to her mother."* We told them not to, then built a mechanic
that required them to. **They obeyed, correctly.**

## Dice that shaped the session more than the design did

**{{pc:2}} rolled very low at the stumble.** It had to be inserted rather than
played from the deck, and it landed weak on purpose: Rosa did not fall, {{pc:2}}
barely registered anything, and afterwards doubted whether she had seen something
or imagined it. **The GM-held line never fired here.**

**{{pc:1}} rolled very low several times running after two hours of spinning**,
walked into the broom closet instead of the kitchen, and spent minutes admiring a
mechanical dust pan with an integrated roller and a carpet beater. **Then rolled
high, came out, and immediately spotted the planners.** Neither half was designed
and the pair of them is the best {{pc:1}} material of the campaign so far.

## The amendment that matters

**Rosa's claim is narrower than it was written, and the played version is canon.**
At the door she said she has **never met another keeper of a ledger** — but that
she has, rarely, **met other people with abilities like the party's**, and never
said what those are.

This supersedes *"she believes she is the only person in the world who does
this."* **It fits her existing peer-network line and costs the arc nothing** — the
ledger still names no other keeper across forty-eight years.
