# The Flight Attendant

## Vestigial variant — this scene may not fire (compressed Ep 2)

A real table (2026-08-15) played the compressed 4-scene Ep 2 in which this scene did **not fire in full**. The party tried to reach Yui at home; nothing much came of the attempt. The Yui emotional-center beat instead came *through Reggie* at the In-N-Out (see [`02-the-gate-agent.md`](02-the-gate-agent.md) §"What Reggie says about Yui") — his agitation about her near-firing landed the human cost without the party ever getting a scene with Yui directly.

**When to run this reduced variant:** the two-stage Scene 2 landed hard on Reggie's Yui-affection beat; the party is short on session time; the table is unlikely to relitigate a doorstep scene that repeats emotional beats already delivered. Skip to Scene 5 (or, in the compressed flow, straight to the Starbucks encounter with Bea — see [`05-the-lead.md`](05-the-lead.md) §"Compressed variant").

**Cost of skipping:** Yui's memory-hole isn't personally witnessed by the party. Her wife Inez and daughter Mei don't appear this episode. The witness/edited asymmetry gets landed less viscerally. Bank this as debt to spend in a later episode where Yui recurs — she's a recurring texture NPC and can carry the beat later when it's needed.

**Otherwise** — run the full scene below, which remains the emotional center of the standard 7-scene Ep 2.

The PCs find **Yui Tanaka** (full sheet: [`../../../characters/npcs/yui-tanaka.json`](../../../characters/npcs/yui-tanaka.json)) near her home in Millbrae (a coffee shop, or her doorstep — where her wife **Inez** may answer; see [`../dm/npcs.md`](../dm/npcs.md)). This is the episode's emotional center.

## The wall (parallel denial)

Yui has no memory of holding the bag either. Her memory is anchored to a pre-edit shape in which the bag-hold never happened; her mind never updated when the world's shape did. She is not lying. But where Reggie was defensive, **Yui is rattled** — the conversation surfaces the mismatch (paperwork and the incident report attest she held the bag; her memory attests she didn't) more sharply for her, because she's the one who was almost fired for something she has no memory of doing. See [`../../001-unattended-baggage/dm/the-cable.md`](../../001-unattended-baggage/dm/the-cable.md) §"record-vs-memory asymmetry" for the mechanism.

> *"I don't — no. I would never take a passenger's bag, that's a fireable — " (she stops) "Why are you asking me this? Did someone say I — " (the cuff tug; her tell) "I've been so tired. Mei's been sick. I've lost whole hours this month. I thought it was just..."*

## The door (the human beat)

She's the one paying the cost of the mismatch, on-screen:

- She's been **written up for an incident her memory doesn't include** (a bag at a gate — possibly a *different* flight; the same impossible shape), and it's threatening the purser application she's been quietly chasing for two years.
- Mei (her three-year-old) was up sick last night; Yui has been running on no sleep and has started to distrust her own mind.

The PCs can be **kind to her or use her.** Kindness costs nothing and pays: she becomes recurring texture (the cost paid by people who never know they paid it — quiet Act II groundwork). If they're cruel or cornering, she shuts down and leaves, shaken.

**Hand the lead to the caregiver PC.** This is the scene to spotlight the caregiver/Medic (their Insight reads Yui's exhaustion and fear; their instinct is to help effectively and gently). Front-loading the caregiver's humanity here — before the louder PCs colonize the session — is a load-bearing table anchor (see [`../dm/README.md`](../dm/README.md)). Cue them: *"You're the one who clocks it — she's not stonewalling, she's scared. What do you do?"*

**The campaign refuses to fix her.** You cannot install a memory of an event the world no longer attests; do not try. The restraint *is* the point.

**Rolls:** **Insight** to read her — Hit (10+): her memory tracks a shape that doesn't include the incident, the mismatch frightens her, and it's costing her (the write-up); Partial (7–9): she's rattled but you can't tell guilt from fear; Miss (≤6): you read only exhaustion. **Influence to push** her: she shuts down regardless of the roll — *"I think you should go."* (Don't reward pushing.)

## The insight to plant (hard)

Across this scene and Reggie's, make sure the table sees the asymmetry, without naming it:

> *The world was edited so that Yui held the bag. Records (camera, paperwork) attest what actually happened in the current shape. Reggie and Yui hold versions of events that don't include the bag-hold — the kind of memory-gap that happens all the time in ordinary human life, notoriously unreliable eyewitness testimony as it is, except that here it's happening in a pattern. Your memories attest what you saw. Both are accurate to what they hold. The mismatch between the records and the actors' memories is what you're looking at.*

This is the specific evidence that primes one PC for the magic-realization beat in sessions 3–5. The natural first-realizer is the PC whose own life is a world-attests-never-existed story; let that player feel it. Do not connect it for them. *(Cosmology precision — the DM must hold this even before sharing it: the world's shape was edited (past-directed self-consistency accommodation); records default to attesting the current shape; memories default to whichever version is cheapest for the substrate to hold. Reggie and Yui's memories don't include the bag-hold because that's the cheapest version for their circumstances — see [`../../001-unattended-baggage/dm/the-cable.md`](../../001-unattended-baggage/dm/the-cable.md) §"Common DM FAQ" for the mechanism and edge cases including conflicting-witness accounts. If the DM teaches "your memory was edited" (memory-as-target), players hunt for a culprit that doesn't exist. If the DM teaches "the world was edited; records default to current shape; memories default to whatever's cheapest to hold, which usually looks like ordinary eyewitness unreliability," players correctly look for the phenomenon.)*

## A possible footage-as-mirror beat (only if footage is already in hand)

If your table got the footage in Scene 4 *before* this scene, Yui watching herself on camera holding a bag she has no memory of holding is a stronger, sadder version of her rattled beat — *shared bewilderment, not a gotcha.* Frame it as two people staring at an impossible fact together, never as the PCs cornering her with proof. (Scene order is flexible; see [`../dm/pacing.md`](../dm/pacing.md).)

## What not to do

- Don't let her partially remember. Same invariant as Reggie.
- Don't let kindness *heal* the mismatch. It can comfort her; it cannot install a memory of an event the world's current shape doesn't include.

## What this scene is doing

Confirming the pattern is not unique to one PC's bad memory; landing the episode's emotional beat; planting the witness / edited-around asymmetry for the realization runway; and banking Yui (and her wife Inez, daughter Mei) as recurring human cost.

## Pacing

~15 minutes. This is not compressible below ~10 without losing the human beat — it's the heart of the session. If time is tight elsewhere, cut Scene 5, not this.
