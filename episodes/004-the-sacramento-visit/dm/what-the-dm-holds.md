## Standing DM knowledge for this session

### What Priya's migraines ARE (DM holds; never voice at the table)

**Body-level substrate-sensitivity.** Priya's care/body domain gives her passive intake for substrate events near her — she registers them as pain in her forehead. Severity is graded:

- **Sub-threshold (a "low hum")**: nearby Cheap-tier casts, especially at close range or in ambient-substrate environments. Priya may not notice, or may notice and not name. Deliver as sensation, not migraine.
- **Migraine (throbbing pressure behind eyes)**: nearby Costly-tier or higher casts, substrate-sensitive person in body-crisis (like Vivian's syncope), ambient substrate over an extended approach (approaching an anchor-adjacent site). Bad enough to send her home from work.
- **Debilitating (nausea, can't function)**: nearby Hard-tier casts or anchor-site permeability events firing loudly. Rare; use once per act at most.

Not stress. Not empathy. Not retro-causal in any meaningful sense.

**Firing rules — keep signals rare and meaningful, not constant.** Fires only when ALL of these hold:

- **Real substrate event nearby** — a Cheap-tier or higher cast by another PC or network NPC, an anchor-site permeability moment (a "thin" location becoming more open), OR a substrate-sensitive person in a body-crisis (like Vivian's syncope). Ambient substrate texture also qualifies (approaching a substrate-adjacent location while it's active).
- **Within perceptual range** — same city block is strong; same city is faint; further away is nothing. She's not a Bay-Area-wide radar.
- **NOT Priya's own casts** — she doesn't feel her own signature.
- **NOT Free-tier casts by anyone** — too small to register (Morgan's shoe-size intuition, tiny "you-just-knew" beats — none of that pings her).
- **NOT stress alone, NOT trauma alone, NOT ambient migraine triggers** — the DM should track these separately if using them for character texture, but they're not substrate signals.

Expected frequency across a campaign: ~1-2 migraines per act (3-6 total). Not constant. Real signal.

**How this works in Ep 4 specifically:**
- **Wednesday/Friday evening flare** (before the session, per pre-session): trigger was a substrate-adjacent event nearby. Pick something concrete the DM tracks privately. (Suggestion: a network caster elsewhere in the Bay Area did a Cheap-tier cast Wednesday night; nothing to do with the party.)
- **Drive migraine** (Scene 2 Beat 1): triggered by Vivian's syncope this morning (substrate-sensitive person's body-state changing) + Priya approaching Vivian's house (ambient substrate — decades of pattern-observation activity soaked in).
- **Threshold release** (Scene 2 Beat 3): she's IN the substrate-adjacent environment now, not near it. Differential drops to zero — no more migraine. Like moving from another room to the source room; the signal becomes the noise floor.

**DM's job:** track Priya's migraine triggers privately across sessions. If in doubt whether a substrate event should fire her migraine, err on the side of NOT firing it — rare and meaningful beats frequent and diluted. Ep 5-6 Phase-2 realization beat is when she articulates the pattern to another PC.

### Mars's neck-flare policy

Mars's neck-flare (per his sheet, the mugging-at-Pier-80 cost signature) fires PROMINENTLY on:
- **His first cast** (this session, if it fires)
- **Costly-tier or higher casts** (future)

For future **Cheap or Free-tier casts**: log privately; mention on-page at DM's discretion, roughly 1 in 3. Overuse tedious; underuse loses the pattern. This session is his first; mention it clearly (see Scene 3 Beat 1).

### Private-delivery protocol for silent player-specific beats

Several beats this session need to be delivered to ONE player privately (Priya's body-recognition; Mars's neck-flare). Pick a protocol before session and use it consistently:

- **Index cards passed at the table** — pre-write the lines, slide them across. Best if you have props already; slowest.
- **Discord DM / Signal DM** — fast if the table is online; needs everyone with a device. Also works face-to-face if all players have phones out.
- **Brief step-aside** — DM stands, addresses one player quietly out of earshot for ~10 sec. Works face-to-face; interrupts flow.
- **Whispered aside at the table** — quickest; risks others overhearing, which breaks the silent-player firewall.

The Ep 4 Scene 3 Beat 1 checklist has THREE private-line deliveries in a 30-second window. If you're using step-aside or whisper, batch them (one after the other, minimize the flow disruption).

### Beat 1 — Anger *(15–20 min; DM reigns in if runaway)* [STRUCTURE M2]

Three ledgers on Dakota's dining table. Kettle on the stove. Party arrives one at a time — awkward hellos. Priya arrives last and stays standing.

**{{pc:4}}'s player plays their chosen stance** — see the DM-only local stance prep doc (Focused / Contrite / Doubling-down / Frightened).

**DM reign-in guidance.** Anger is real and gets played, but it must not derail the episode start. If the room is escalating past 20 min OR players are heating up out-of-character, **DM prompts the pivot**: *"OK — you can be angry, and you're still all here. What do you want to know?"* This is a legitimate DM tool, not railroading. Priya will most naturally hold anger longest — that's fine; she can carry it into the drive.

**Live-DM flag: Mira break.** If Mira breaks her silence with Dakota during this beat ({{pc:3}}'s player's play choice), it's a first: previously-silent PC directly contradicts Dakota. Actionable moves for the DM if it fires:
- **Slow the moment.** Give the room 3-5 seconds. Let it land.
- **{{pc:4}}'s player (as Dakota) reacts per his chosen stance** — Focused/Doubling-down likely defensiveness; Contrite may soften and thank her; Frightened may take it as validation. Don't scripted-force; let {{pc:4}}'s player play.
- **Watch for other PCs to weigh in.** Priya likely amps the confrontation; Morgan may quietly support Mira; Mars often stays back but might step forward.
- **Do NOT rush to resolution.** The anger is real and the break is real.
- **Log it.** {{pc:3}}'s player having Mira contradict Dakota publicly matters for future arcs (log to DM's private play-state notes after session). It shifts the party dynamic Dakota can no longer count on.

## Scene 3 — Vivian's House (~75 min)

**Location:** Vivian Loewe's 1960s Sacramento suburban ranch. **Budget:** 75 min (70–85 realistic).

### Vivian's active want (DM holds — engine of the scene)

Vivian is 98 (born 1928). Her father Dr. Erik Loewe led a classified team at Field Annex 7, Bodie 1942–1948 — cybernetics-adjacent research studying statistical anomalies in historical records, under War Department cover. Vivian was 14–20 during his tenure; joined summer trips as a child and worked briefly as a young hire in the annex's last two years (1946–1948). Her father died in 1961 asking her to keep his work private. She has kept the promise for 65 years.

**She is dying now. She wants to be released from the promise before she goes.** The party's arrival is not "answering a question" — it is her **assaying whether they are trustworthy enough that she can speak.** She is warm, sharp, and testing them.

**The party can fail this test.** If they push too hard too fast, or lie obviously, she withholds the elder-witness line, the Bea-callback, and the closing warning-with-glance-at-Dakota. They leave with the notebook and Rosa's name but not the deeper disclosure.

### Playable cue for the assay *(early in Beat 2)*

So players can *feel* the test without being invisibly gated:

> *"Sit with me a moment. I want to know how you talk to each other before we talk about my father."*

Then Vivian asks each PC one small personal question and listens. Attentive players will feel her weighing them.

### Beat 0 — Arrival *(5 min)* [SCENE-DRIVER]

**Mira's outline-vision on the house exterior [SCENE-DRIVER — Mira agency].** Before they knock, Mira sits with the house for a moment. She reads the outline of **one specific removed object** — a wheelchair ramp that was built and later taken down (Vivian's late husband, decades ago; the concrete pad is still where the ramp was). Not "the emotional shape of family" — one physical object, present-in-absence. **DM holds a beat of silence when this lands.**

### Beat 1 — Elena at the door + Mars's first cast *(15 min)* [STRUCTURE]

**Elena** opens the door — late 30s / early 40s, professional, protective. Two-year-plus caregiver for Vivian; previously worked hospice. Met Dakota Thursday, liked him.

**Elena's setup:** Vivian had a syncope episode about an hour ago. Elena wanted to call an ambulance; Vivian refused. Elena is holding it (adrenaline down, decision-fatigue up). She's the one who was going to call and reschedule; the party arrived unannounced before she got the chance.

**Read-aloud (Elena's opening):**

> *"Oh — you're back. She's been asking when you'd call. Come in, come — "* (she registers the four unfamiliar faces behind Dakota and pauses; the warmth doesn't leave her face but her posture shifts, more careful) *"...oh. All of you. Today. She had a rough morning. I was about to call and push this to tomorrow — didn't realize you were already on your way. Is one of you medical?"*

**Priya will most naturally offer credentials here.** NICU nurse, UCSF. Elena's shoulders drop slightly — a nurse in the room changes the risk calculation. She's thinking she can let Dakota + Priya in for a short visit; the other three can wait outside or come back tomorrow.

**Now the party needs to ask her to admit all five together.** This is the Mars-cast moment.

#### Mars's presence-cast — DM's escalating nudges

Mars's cast will fire on {{pc:5}}'s player describing **both stance AND intent**. But do not depend on {{pc:5}}'s player spontaneously producing that — presence-domain casts look "just like being charismatic" from the outside, and {{pc:5}}'s player has no in-fiction cue that he can do anything special here. **The DM guides him in.**

**Escalation ladder — deploy in order, stop when it lands:**

**1. Wait 10 seconds after Elena's speech.** Silence. See if any player pushes for "all five, please." If {{pc:5}}'s player speaks first, go to step 4. If someone else speaks (Priya "please"), let it play; Elena softens but doesn't reverse.

**2. Look at {{pc:5}}'s player. Say directly: *"Mars is standing right there. What does he do?"*** (Address by their actual name when running.)

If {{pc:5}}'s player says something character-appropriate (any level of specificity), go to step 4.

**3. If {{pc:5}}'s player's response is generic ("I don't know" or "he just stands there"), prompt for specifics:**

> *"How's Mars standing right now? What's he thinking?"*

That's a legitimate table-craft question. Not a tell.

**4. Once {{pc:5}}'s player produces stance + intent (naturally or via prompting), the cast fires.**

**Rule of thumb for the DM's live judgment: intent = a WHY, not a WHAT.** Interior reasoning or motive — what Mars is trying to accomplish or hold — not physical description. If {{pc:5}}'s player tells you what Mars is doing but not why, that's stance without intent.

Examples that qualify:
- *"Mars stands with his hands loose, back on his heels, doesn't crowd her, doesn't look past her — just waits. He's thinking: we drove up together; we should go in together."*
- *"Mars steps back half a foot, opens his stance, quiets his shoulders. He's not trying to convince Elena; he's trying to be four people she doesn't need to worry about instead of one."*

**5. If {{pc:5}}'s player's first response is stance-only, back up to step 3 and prompt for interior** — don't drop straight to fallback. The escalation ladder is for helping {{pc:5}}'s player produce the beat, not punishing him for not producing it spontaneously.

**6. If steps 1-5 still don't produce stance + intent** — offer one more move: *"{{pc:5}}'s player, describe how Mars stands, and I'll narrate his reasoning as something you can accept or reject."* This preserves {{pc:5}}'s player's authorship of Mars while unlocking the cast. {{pc:5}}'s player says yes → cast fires with DM-narrated intent that {{pc:5}}'s player confirms. {{pc:5}}'s player says no → fallback (Elena admits Dakota + Priya only; see below).

**Do NOT explain the intent requirement to {{pc:5}}'s player. Ever.** If he asks why Elena softened, say *"You held the room."*

#### The moment of cast — deliver in this sequence, IMMEDIATELY

Once Mars gets stance + intent:

**A. Elena's line.** Deliver at natural conversational pace:

> *"OK. You,"* (to Dakota) *"and you"* (to Priya) *"— you can come in for a short one. The rest — I'm sorry, I can't do five right now. Can the other three come back — "* (small mid-word pause; she looks at Mars, then past him at the two behind him, then back at Mars) *"...actually. All of you. Twenty minutes, and if she asks you to leave, you leave. Come in the entryway; wait there while I check on her."*

Elena was about to say *"come back tomorrow."* She said *"actually. All of you"* instead. She doesn't feel disoriented — just the ordinary experience of hearing yourself soften on a boundary you were about to hold. Ten seconds later she has a story about it (Dakota's calm return, the nurse in the group, Vivian did say "with the others"). The story is fine. Substrate is happy.

**B. Priya's body-recognition** — deliver ONE line to {{pc:2}}'s player alone, privately. This is the **sub-threshold "low hum" variant** (Mars's cast is Cheap-tier + Priya is in an ambient-substrate environment); not a migraine. Keep the callback narrow — just the driveway release:

> *"Priya — as Mars settles into that stance, you feel that low hum at the back of your skull again. Same one you couldn't quite place at the driveway earlier. You don't know what to make of it."*

{{pc:2}}'s player may share, deflect, or sit on it. All correct. Do NOT reference the Union Hall fire from Ep 3 — that would tip the migraine mechanism per Ep 3 §14 firewall; save cross-session callbacks for the Phase-2 realization beat itself.

**C. Mars's neck-flare** — deliver ONE line to {{pc:5}}'s player alone, privately.
