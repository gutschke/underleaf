# DM Cheat Sheet — Ep 3 "The Arrested Town"
*Print single page. Keep at hand. Do not read at the table.*

---

## The three lines you must have memorized

1. **On "is this magic?"** → *"Could be. Could not be. What does your character do?"*
2. **On "so what does it MEAN?"** → *"What do you think it means?"* (then listen)
3. **On "AI? Conspiracy?"** → **same as #1** — *"Could be. Could not be."* Do NOT affirm; the theory must come from the players' reasoning, never from the DM.

## Never say these words at the table

`magic` · `The Quiet` · `casting` · `cast` · `spell` · `premonition` · `psychic` · `chosen` · `destiny` · `awakening` · `the elect` · `sixth sense`

If a player says one, echo back an inert word: *"lucky"*, *"weird"*, *"a coincidence"*.

## The load-bearing beats

- **Federal-annex brochure line** — read verbatim to at least two PCs. Let them notice independently. *"Select outbuildings were requisitioned by federal agencies between 1942 and 1948 for classified purposes; records remain sealed."*
- **{{pc:4}}'s "Yes" text** — arrives simultaneously on all four phones, right after the reunion

## The wrong-right theory (feed, don't confirm)

The PCs will land on *"someone is covering something up, maybe with AI/tech."* This is the theory you want them to hold for another 2-3 sessions. Feed it warmly. Never confirm.

## Accidental cast quick-reference

**Who is the cast most likely to lean into?** Ranked — but read the room, not the doc. Take whichever candidate opens organically.
1. **{{pc:2}}** — body/care domain; keep-us-warm instinct
2. **{{pc:3}}** — memory/identity; "she remembers where wood was kept"
3. **{{pc:1}}** — systems/information; long shot for cold
4. **{{pc:5}}** — posture/presence; needs a social frame

**Grant it Free-tier** — no roll, no stress. Something concrete happens.

**When another PC says "do it again"** — the caster TRIES. Nothing happens. This is the first taste of the asymmetry between accidental and willed magic. Do not narrate this as a *failure* — narrate it as *"the thing is over. You reach for it and it's not there."*
